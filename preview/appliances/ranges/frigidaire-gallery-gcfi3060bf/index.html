Highlights

  • Total Convection System
    Get over 15 ways to cook - Total Convection powers cooking modes like Air Fry, No Preheat, Slow Cook, Convection Bake, Steam Bake, Air Sous Vide, Bread Proof, Dehydrate and more!
  • Easy-to-Clean Cooktop
    Unlike traditional electric and gas cooktops, induction only heats the pan and the food in it, so the area around the pan stays cooler, allowing you to clean up spills and splatters while you cook.
  • No Preheat
    Eliminates time wasted waiting on your oven to heat up - simply place your food in the cold oven and get to mealtime, in no time.
  • Air Fry
    Make healthier and delicious meals for your entire family - right in your oven.
  • Slow Cook
    With Slow Cook, your food cooks slowly and at a lower temperature (as low as 100° F), allowing you to lock in moisture and flavor without over or undercooking your food.
  • Faster Boil Times
    Heat transfers directly to the cookware bypassing the cooktop surface, making induction incredibly fast.
