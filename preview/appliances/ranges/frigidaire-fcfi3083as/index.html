Highlights

  • Easy-to-Clean Cooktop
    Unlike traditional electric and gas cooktops, induction only heats the pan and the food in it, so the area around the pan stays cooler to the touch, allowing you to clean up spills and splatters while you cook.
  • Convection Bake
    Get faster, more even baking results with our fan convection baking system.
  • Faster Boil Times
    Induction boils water 50% faster than gas or electric cooktops.
  • Bridge Element
    Connects two elements to create a larger cooking surface, perfect for a griddle or large pan.
  • One-Touch Self Clean
    A convenient 3 hour self clean cycle cleans your oven so you don't have to.
  • Consistent results every time
    Induction delivers steady, even heat across the surface of your pan so you get consistent results every time.
