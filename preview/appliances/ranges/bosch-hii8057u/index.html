Highlights

  • Self-Clean Mode for convenience
    With all the cooking you'll do in a Bosch oven, clean-up should be your last worry.
  • Faster cooking with induction
    When you place a pan on an induction cooktop and turn it on, an electric current travels through a coiled copper wire, which produces and applies heat directly to your pan.
  • Easy installation
    The slide-in design can replace a freestanding range cutout and slides back against the wall for a sleek built-in look.
  • Warming Drawer for convenient storage
    A Warming Drawer beneath the oven provides extra storage, and keeps your food warm until it's ready to serve.

    A third heating element in the back and a fan circulates heat evenly so you get evenly golden results no matter where you place your pan.
