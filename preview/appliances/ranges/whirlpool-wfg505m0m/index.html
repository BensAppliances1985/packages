Highlights

  • Keep Warm setting
    Keep dishes from getting cold with a setting that delivers low heat to the oven, so your meal is ready when you are.
  • Two SpeedHeat™ Burners
    Get dinner to the table fast with two SpeedHeat™ Burners that generate the high heat needed to sear and boil quickly
  • Frozen Bake™ Technology
    Skip preheating and cook frozen favorites like pizza and lasagna in fewer steps with preprogrammed settings.
  • Dishwasher-Safe Cast-Iron Grates
    Wash these cast-iron grates right in the dishwasher when they get messy, thanks to a dishwasher-safe finish that makes them easy to clean.
  • Edge-to-Edge Cast-Iron Grates
    Get more space to sauté, boil and simmer with cast-iron grates that extend across the entire cooktop, giving you the flexibility to place pots and pans anywhere.
  • Broiler Drawer
    Use the high heat of the Broiler Drawer to quickly melt or brown your favorite dishes in a separate cavity while you cook another dish in the main oven.
