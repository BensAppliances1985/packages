Highlights

  • Total Convection System
    Gives you maximum versatility in the kitchen, powering cooking modes like Air Fry, No Preheat, Slow Cook, Convection Bake, Steam Bake, Convection Roast, Steam Roast and more!
  • Easy-to-Clean Cooktop
    Unlike traditional electric and gas cooktops, Induction only heats the pan and the food in it, so the area around the pan stays cooler, allowing you to clean up spills and splatters while you cook.
  • No Preheat
    Eliminates time wasted waiting on your oven to heat up - simply place your food in the cold oven and get to meal time, in no time.
  • ReadyCook™ Air Fry Tray is included
    For perfectly crispy and delicious Air Fry results.
  • Faster Boil Times
    With induction, heat transfers directly to the cookware bypassing the cooktop surface, making induction incredibly fast.
  • Temperature Probe
    Monitors and alerts when the desired finished cooking temperature has been reached.
