Highlights

  • Air Fry
    Make healthier and delicious meals for your entire family - right in your oven.
  • Slow Cook
    Your food cooks slowly and at a lower temperature (as low as 100° F), allowing you to lock in moisture and flavor without over or undercooking your food.
  • Total Convection System
    Gives you maximum versatility in the kitchen, powering cooking modes like Air Fry, Slow Cook, Convection Bake, Steam Bake, Convection Roast, Steam Roast, Bread Proof, Dehydrate and more!
  • Six Burner Cooktop
    Six burner cooktop provides the ultimate cooking flexibility, including two powerful 18,000-BTU Quick Boil Burners, two 16,000-BTU burners and a 5,000-BTU simmer.
  • Temperature Probe
    Monitors and alerts when the desired finished cooking temperature has been reached.

    Select Steam Clean for a quick, 30 minute clean or choose Self Clean, which is available in 2, 3 and 4 hour cycles.
