Highlights

  • Air Fry
    Air Fry allows you to make healthier and delicious meals for the entire family, right in your oven.
  • Total Convection System
    Total Convection gives you maximum versatility in the kitchen, powering cooking modes like Air Fry, No Preheat, Slow Cook, Air Sous Vide and more!
  • No Preheat
    Eliminates time wasted waiting on your oven to heat up - simply place your food in the cold oven and get to mealtime, in no time.
  • Five Element Cooktop
    Designed to handle all of your cooking needs from a powerful 3,200-Watt EvenTemp® element to a 100W Keep Warm element.
  • Slow Cook
    With Slow Cook, your food cooks slowly and at a lower temperature (as low as 100°F), allowing you to lock in moisture and flavor without over or undercooking your food.
  • Air Sous Vide
    Uses precisely controlled temperature and airflow to lock in flavor and moisture to cook foods to the finish temperature you want.
