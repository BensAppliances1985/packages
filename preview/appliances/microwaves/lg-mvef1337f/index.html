Highlights

  • Enjoy Perfectly Cooked Meals with Sensor Cook
    Select from a menu of sensor cooking options and let the microwave determine when your food is ready using pre-programmed settings and humidity-sensing technology.
  • Convenient Auto Cook Modes
    Make cooking easier and decisions faster by selecting from a menu of pre-programmed, automatic cooking settings for tasty meals.
  • Remove Smoke and Odors with 4-Speed, 550 CFM Ventilation
    4-Speed ventilation with 550 CFM power helps remove smoke, steam, and odors from your kitchen.2
  • Slim, Low-Profile Design. Max Height.
    The slim, unobtrusive design provides a sleek look and a surprisingly roomy interior that offers the most capacity and usable height in its class.¹ So, you can fit taller bowls, mugs, and dishes up to 7.7 inches tall with ease.
  • Zero Clearance Door for Flush Installation
    Thanks to the Zero Clearance Door, the microwave can be installed flush against a wall or cabinet without the need for extra clearance space for the door to swing open.
  • Sleek SmoothTouch® Glass Controls
    The intuitive SmoothTouch® glass controls deliver sleek style and easy operation, with just a touch of your finger.
