Highlights

  • Heated Dry with fan
    Increased temperature and a powerful internal fan help reduce excess moisture inside your dishwasher to get dishes nice and dry.**Compared to Short Heated Dry.
  • Enhanced Wash
    A wash and dry performance cycle that uses more water and heat than the Normal cycle, delivering a more powerful clean.
  • Fingerprint resistant stainless steel
    Easy-to-clean, fingerprint resistant stainless steel resists smudges and streaks to keep your dishwasher looking as good on the outside as it works on the inside.
  • Adjustable upper rack
    Put bulky pots and pans in their place with an adjustable upper rack that fits big dishes usually left for the bottom—so there’s no need for a second load.
  • Sani Rinse
    The Sani Rinse option lets you confidently wash important items with a high-temperature rinse that removes 99.999% of food soil bacteria.

    Worry less about your dishwasher overfilling with a sensor that will detect if too much water is in the tub and cut the power to the inlet valve.
