Highlights

  • Triple Filtration System
    Uses three levels of filtration to remove food particles during the wash and rinse cycles.
  • Flush Design
    Allows dishwasher to fit evenly with countertops and cabinetry to help give the kitchen a seamless look and feel.
  • Door-Open Dry System
    Automatically opens after the cycle to allow hot air out and fresh air to circulate within, making it KitchenAid brand's most energy efficient dry system.* *Compared to KitchenAid® Dishwashers with ProDry™ Option.
  • Pre-Rinse Option
    Helps overcome the need to hand rinse before loading the dishwasher by washing away small easy-to-remove soils for optimized cleaning performance during the main wash cycle.
  • 14 Place Settings
    Fit in the dishwasher all at once with two full levels of adjustable rack space that make room for your pots, pans, glassware and plates.
  • Wash Cycle Countdown
    Conveniently projects an easily readable LED countdown below the dishwasher onto the floor, so you always know the status of the cycle and how much time is left.
