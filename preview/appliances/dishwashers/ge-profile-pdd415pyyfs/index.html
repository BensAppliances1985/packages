Highlights

  • Two Dishwashers in One
    Two drawers that run separately or together give you more control to clean the way you want—whether it’s a quick rinse or a full party’s worth of dishes.
  • Fits a Standard 24" Dishwasher Cabinet
    This Double Drawer Dishwasher is designed to be installed flush with your cabinets and fits into a standard 24" dishwasher cutout ensuring it blends seamlessly into your kitchen.
  • Conveniently Wash Small Loads for Everyday Use
    Wash in just one drawer to save time, water and energy so you always have clean dishes available when you need them.

    This dishwasher drawer design makes daily loading and unloading of your dishes effortless with waist-level access and less bending.
