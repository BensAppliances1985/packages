Highlights

  • Smart Wall Oven
    When you’re busy elsewhere, use your smartphone to get dinner started by remotely preheating the oven.
  • Small Space Solution
    Fit your own casserole dishes or bakeware in a wall oven that takes up less space and still gives you the flexibility to cook on multiple racks at once.
  • 9-in-1 Oven with True Convection
    Cook however you want with 9 cooking modes in 1 oven: Frozen Bake™ Technology, Bake, Convect Bake, Broil, Convect Broil, Defrost, Keep Warm, Proofing and Convect Roast cycles.
  • Assisted Cooking
    Assisted Cooking cycles calculate and set the ideal cooking time and temperature for common foods like casseroles, meat, fish, vegetables, pizza and some baked goods.
  • 2.9 Cu. Ft. Capacity
    Make large family meals, even in a small kitchen, with a 2.9 Cu. Ft. Capacity oven that can fit your main course and side dish.
  • True Convection with Third Heating Element
    True convection cooking uses the bake element, the broil element, a third heating element and a fan in the back of the oven to bake multiple items at once and circulate hot air around the oven cavity for more even heating.
