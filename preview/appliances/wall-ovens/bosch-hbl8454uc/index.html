Highlights

  • Support heavy dishes with a Telescopic Rack
    The EasyGlide™ telescopic rack moves smoothly and supports heavy dishes and casserole pans with ease, making it effortless to pull out the racks.
  • A QuietClose Door that never slams
    Dampened hinges softly guide the QuietClose® Door shut for a more peaceful kitchen.
  • Integrated Air Fryer in your Oven
    Air Fry uses convection fans to rapidly circulate dry, hot air so you can achieve crispy, delicious results — without the use of added oils in traditional frying.
  • Convection Multi-Rack for even baking on all racks
    Convection Multi-Rack (Convection Pro) has a third heating element and a fan (regular Convection only has two heating elements) evenly circulates air around the cavity so you get consistently even golden results.
  • Grill inside with the Broil mode
    Broil mode uses intense heat radiated from the upper element and delivers the perfect char, sear, and grill without the mess.
  • Self-Clean Mode for convenience
    With all the cooking you'll do in a Bosch oven, clean-up should be your last worry.
