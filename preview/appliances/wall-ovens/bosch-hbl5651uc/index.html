Highlights

  • Metal knobs for stylish control
    Ovens feature heavy duty metal knobs for temperature changes and cooking modes.
  • The ultimate cooking space
    Twice the space and capacity.
  • Convection Multi-Rack (Convection Pro) for even baking on all racks
    Convection Multi-Rack has a third heating element and a fan (regular Convection only has two heating elements) evenly circulates air around the cavity so you get consistently even golden results.
  • Fully flush installation
    All Bosch wall ovens install completely flush with your cabinetry, so they blend in beautifully and never protrude.

    Pizza mode mimics the intense heat of a pizza oven to deliver artisanal pies in your kitchen.
