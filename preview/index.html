How packages are picked

  • All four appliances are one brand and one finish, so the kitchen matches. GE Profile, Frigidaire Gallery and LG STUDIO count as their own brands.
  • Each appliance's score (0 to 100) blends owner ratings pooled across major retailers with repair-technician reliability rankings and other research we track. A package's score is the average of its four appliances.
  • Every model has at least 250 owner reviews, every fridge holds at least 17.5 cu ft, and every dishwasher and microwave is installed (no portables).
  • Prices are Lowe's prices, including current sales. Bundle savings follow Lowe's tiers for the eligible models in the package.
