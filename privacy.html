Privacy

Last updated 2026-11-28. This page covers Kitchen Package Builder, run by Ben's Appliances.

What we collect

Nothing you type. The site has no accounts, sign-ups or forms, and your filter choices stay in your browser.

Analytics

We use Google Analytics to count visits and see which appliances people look at and click through to a retailer. Google Analytics sets cookies and collects information such as the pages you view, the links you click, your device and browser, and your approximate location. It does not give us your name or contact details. Google's handling of this data is described in Google's privacy policy, and you can opt out with Google's browser add-on.

Retailer links and commissions

We earn a commission on purchases through our links. Scores are our own. Links to retailers go through their affiliate programs:

  • Lowe's, through Lowe's Creator (shoplowes.me and creatoriq.cc links).
  • Best Buy, through Best Buy Creators on Impact.
  • AJ Madison, through CJ Affiliate.

When you click one, the retailer and the company that runs its affiliate program may set cookies so a purchase you make within their tracking window can be credited to us. Those cookies are theirs, and each retailer's privacy policy covers them. The commission doesn't change the price you pay, and retailers have no say in our scores or rankings.

Prices and product information

Prices and stock come from each retailer's public listings and product feeds, refreshed weekly. Lowe's prices are for one Columbus, Ohio area store. Prices and stock can change at any time, so please confirm with the retailer before you buy. Product photos and specifications come from the manufacturers, via Skulytics.
