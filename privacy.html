Privacy

Last updated 2026-10-20. This page covers Kitchen Package Builder, run by Ben's Appliances.

What we collect

Nothing you type. The site has no accounts, sign-ups or forms, and your budget and filter choices stay in your browser.

Analytics

We use Google Analytics to count visits and see which budgets, brands and packages people look at and click through to the retailer. Google Analytics sets cookies and collects information such as the pages you view, the links you click, your device and browser, and your approximate location. It does not give us your name or contact details. Google's handling of this data is described in Google's privacy policy, and you can opt out with Google's browser add-on.

Retailer links and commissions

When you shop through retailer links on our site, we earn a commission. Scores are our own. Links to Lowe's go through Lowe's affiliate program (shoplowes.me). When you click one, Lowe's and the companies that run its affiliate program may set cookies so a purchase you make at Lowe's within the following weeks can be credited to us. Those cookies are theirs, and Lowe's privacy policy covers them. The commission doesn't change the price you pay, and retailers have no say in our scores or rankings.

Prices and product information

Prices, stock and bundle offers come from Lowe's public listings for one Columbus, Ohio area store, refreshed weekly. Your store's price and stock may differ, so please confirm at Lowe's before you buy.
